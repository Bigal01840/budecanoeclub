Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience to join Bude Canoe Club?

No. Everyone has to start somewhere. Our General Skills sessions are open to all abilities, including complete beginners, and our friendly coaches will help you where you need it.

How old do you have to be?

The minimum age is 5 years old and there is no maximum age. If you have an under 16 looking to join, please message us first to talk about their swimming and paddling ability.

Is the first session free?

Yes. Your first session is always free, and you are welcome to bring a friend to try it out too.

How much does it cost?

Pay as you go sessions are £12 per person per session. The All Inclusive Membership is £25 per month for adults, or £15 per month for juniors (18 and under) and concessions (60 and over). Under 18s get the concession rate.

How do I book?

Sign up to our Spond page (Spond) and drop us a message to let us know you would like to come along.

What do I need to bring?

Just suitable swimwear. We provide all the kit and boats you need to get on the water and stay safe.

When and where are sessions?

Winter pool sessions run every Wednesday 7pm – 9pm from October to March at Holsworthy Leisure Centre swimming pool (EX22 6LB). Summer sea and canal sessions run every Wednesday 6pm – 8pm from April to September, meeting at Summerleaze Beach Car Park in Bude (EX23 8HN), by the club kayak trailer near the Lifeboat Station.

Do you play canoe polo?

Yes. Canoe polo runs every Wednesday 8pm – 9pm during winter pool sessions and fortnightly on Mondays in summer on Bude Canal. Our Bude Smugglers team enters tournaments throughout the year, and we host the Andy Hambley Trophy every August Bank Holiday Monday.

Can I learn surf kayaking?

Yes. The club has produced world class surf kayakers, and our coaches include British and World Surf Kayaking Champions.

Are your coaches qualified?

Yes. Our coaches are Paddle UK qualified, our volunteers are DBS checked, the club is insured, and we are affiliated to Paddle UK.

Do I need Paddle UK membership?

It is recommended, and it is required for all surf and polo competitions. It gives you personal paddling insurance and a Waterways Licence.

Which areas does the club cover?

Our paddlers come from across North Cornwall and West Devon, including Bude, Holsworthy, Stratton, Widemouth Bay, Kilkhampton, Morwenstow, Marhamchurch, Bridgerule, Bradworthy, Hartland, Boscastle, Camelford, Launceston, Week St Mary, Shebbear, Hatherleigh, Okehampton, Bideford, Great Torrington.

Is Bude Canoe Club a charity?

Bude Canoe Club is a Community Amateur Sports Club (CASC), run entirely by volunteers.

How do I contact the club?

Email budecanoeclub@gmail.com, or message us on Instagram (@bude_canoe_club) or Facebook.
